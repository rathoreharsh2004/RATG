Our Craftsmanship

The Art of Heritage

From flooring to ceiling, lighting to furniture — delivering a complete royal palace-style heritage experience.

Heritage Dodi Entrances

A grand traditional heritage dodi (main entrance gate) constructed at the entrance, creating a royal first impression.

Jharokhas & Mehrabs

Designing and executing traditional elements like Jharokhas, Torans, Chhatris, Domes, Jalis, Mehrabs, and Baradaris.

Thekri Glass Work

Intricate glass inlay work on interior ceilings, decorative aaliya niches, and heritage-style bar areas enhancing light reflection.

Mor Pankh Ceilings

Ceilings detailed with Mor Pankh (peacock feather) artwork alongside authentic Pipal Patti and Tordi craftsmanship.

Ghokda-Style Domes

Terrace-level domes constructed in traditional style to enhance architectural elegance and authentic heritage character.

Carved Heritage Furniture

Custom-designed antique finish furniture, carved beds, traditional doors, and royal seating configurations.

Royal Jhomer Chandeliers

Statement lighting elements including royal jhomer chandeliers, hanging lights, and antique wall fixtures.

Marble Inlay & Khajur Patti

Premium white marble flooring with borders finished in Khajur Patti detailing, marble inlay, and Patang patterns.

Udaipur · Heritage Haveli Construction

Oladar Haveli

The Oladar Haveli project has been designed and executed in a complete traditional heritage style, ensuring modern structural strength, quality control, and durability from foundation to final finishing. Featuring a grand traditional heritage Dodi entrance, intricate Thekri glass work on ceilings and aaliya niches, Mor Pankh themed artwork with Pipal Patti and Tordi detailing, Ghokda-style terrace domes, and premium white marble flooring with Khajur Patti borders.

Udaipur · Luxury Heritage Villa

Roop Mahal

Roop Mahal is a magnificent heritage-style project inspired by the timeless architecture of Rajasthan. The exterior showcases beautifully crafted heritage-style windows, traditional arches, and elegant façade detailing that reflects the charm of royal havelis. The space highlights intricate glass inlay work, artistic carvings, and classic heritage furniture that enhance the royal character of the project.

Udaipur · Heritage Style Villa / Farmhouse

Mohan Villa

Mohan Villa is a luxurious heritage-style villa inspired by the traditional architecture of Rajasthani havelis. The design showcases beautifully carved arches, decorative wall artwork, and vibrant stained glass windows that enhance natural light and create a royal ambiance. The interiors feature intricately crafted heritage furniture with an antique silver finish, adding a palace-like elegance to the living spaces.

Udaipur · Premium Commercial Interior

First Impression Salon

First Impression Salon is a luxurious heritage-themed salon inspired by the royal charm of Rajasthan. The space features heritage-style interiors, traditional furniture, Belgium glass work, and intricate Thekri glass work, reflecting authentic royal craftsmanship. Classic detailing, artistic elements, and elegant wordings enhance the regal ambiance, creating a premium grooming space with a true touch of heritage elegance.
